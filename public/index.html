How verification works here

Only one UPI payment is ACTIVE at a time, so two ₹49 payments can't be mixed up. Others wait in a queue. A payment is confirmed only when a bank SMS / Gmail alert with the exact amount arrives (try it from Admin → Simulate bank alert), or the merchant confirms a cash/card counter sale. SMS/email matching is a helpful signal, not bank settlement proof — reconcile with your statement.
